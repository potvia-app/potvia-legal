Privacy Policy

Potvia · Effective 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Potvia (“the App”) handles information on your device. Potvia is a local-first ledger for indoor vessel and tray pairings and display moves.

1. Summary

Potvia does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync. Records you create stay in the App’s on-device sandbox unless you choose to export or share them. There is no Potvia business server that receives your vessel notes or photos.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Vessel and tray labels, optional millimeter notes, placement aliases, pairing events, and placement events
  • Optional reference photos you capture with the camera or select with the system photo picker
  • Local schema metadata, revision numbers, and draft or archived lifecycle state

Missing measurements remain blank or null. The App does not fill unknown values with zero for convenience.

3. Accounts and identifiers

Potvia does not offer sign-in, Sign in with Apple, passwords, or profile accounts. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not read the advertising identifier for analytics or ads.

4. Permissions

  • Camera. Requested only when you tap Take Photo to attach an optional reference image. You may refuse and still save text-only records or use Choose Photos.
  • Photos. Potvia uses the system photo picker for images you select. The App does not request full photo-library access and does not write exports into your Photos library.

Potvia does not request location, contacts, microphone, speech recognition, HealthKit, Bluetooth, calendar write access, or push notification authorization in the current version.

5. Exports and sharing

Export is optional and user-initiated. When you generate an archive, you preview scope and may include selected media. Files you save or share outside Potvia (for example through the system share sheet) cannot be recalled by the App. Canceling export does not delete your original ledger.

6. Analytics and third parties

The App does not include third-party analytics SDKs that upload private record content. Opening this Privacy Policy or Support page uses your system browser and is subject to that browser’s own network behavior.

7. Retention and deletion

You can archive or permanently delete vessels and related events inside the App. Uninstalling Potvia or disabling device backup may permanently remove local data. System backups follow your device settings; Potvia does not promise automatic recovery after device loss.

8. Children

Potvia is intended for general audiences managing household plant displays. Do not provide the App to children in a way that conflicts with applicable law in your region.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Potvia, use the in-app Support entry on the Settings screen.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published on this page.

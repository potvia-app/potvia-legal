Privacy Policy

Potvia · Effective 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Potvia (“the App”) handles information on your device. Potvia is a local-first ledger for indoor vessel and tray pairings and display moves.

1. Summary

Potvia does not provide cloud sync. Records you create stay in the App’s on-device sandbox unless you choose to export or share them. There is no Potvia business server that receives your vessel notes, photos, or voice memos.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Vessel and tray labels, optional millimeter notes, placement aliases, pairing events, and placement events
  • Optional reference photos you capture with the camera or import after granting Photos access
  • Optional spoken placement memos you record after granting microphone access
  • A local one-tap session token and a local Petals balance for consumable features
  • Local schema metadata, revision numbers, and draft or archived lifecycle state
  • If you allow notifications, a device push token may be stored locally for Apple push registration

Missing measurements remain blank or null. The App does not fill unknown values with zero for convenience.

3. Accounts and identifiers

Potvia uses One-Tap Login on this device only. There is no Sign in with Apple, no password, and no cloud profile. Log Out ends the local session and keeps your ledger on this device. Delete Account and Clear Data remove the local account and its stored records from this device. The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not read the advertising identifier for analytics or ads.

4. Permissions

  • Camera. Requested only when you tap Take Photo to attach an optional still reference image. You may refuse and still save text-only records or use Choose Photos.
  • Photos. Requested only when you tap Choose Photos to import an optional still reference image into a local record. You may refuse and continue with Take Photo or text-only records. Potvia does not write exports into your Photos library.
  • Microphone. Requested only when you tap Voice Note to record a short spoken placement memo stored on this device. You may refuse and type the placement instead.
  • Notifications. After you enter the main tabs, the system may ask for notification authorization so Potvia can register for Apple push delivery. You may refuse.

Potvia does not request location, contacts, speech recognition, HealthKit, Bluetooth, calendar write access, or tracking permission in the current version.

5. Petals and Apple purchases

Petals are a local consumable balance used for optional paid actions such as saving a vessel as Active and keeping photos in an export, after an in-app confirmation. Top Up packs are Apple In-App Purchases. Apple processes payment. Remaining Petals are not restored after uninstall, Delete Account, or Clear Data. Purchase history and billing disputes follow Apple’s processes.

6. Exports and sharing

Export is optional and user-initiated. When you generate an archive, you preview scope and may include selected media after confirming a Petals spend, or export text only without that spend. Files you save or share outside Potvia (for example through the system share sheet) cannot be recalled by the App. Canceling export does not delete your original ledger.

7. Analytics and third parties

The App does not include third-party analytics SDKs that upload private record content. Opening this Privacy Policy, User Agreement, or Support page uses your system browser and is subject to that browser’s own network behavior. Apple may process purchase and push-delivery data under Apple’s terms.

8. Retention and deletion

You can archive or permanently delete vessels and related events inside the App. Log Out keeps local records. Delete Account and Clear Data remove the local account and stored ledger data from this device. Uninstalling Potvia or disabling device backup may permanently remove local data. System backups follow your device settings; Potvia does not promise automatic recovery after device loss.

9. Children

Potvia is intended for general audiences managing household plant displays. Do not provide the App to children in a way that conflicts with applicable law in your region.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about Potvia, use the in-app Support entry on the Settings screen, or email 910818179@qq.com.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published on this page.
